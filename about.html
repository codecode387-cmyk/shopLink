About ShopLink NG

We help small Nigerian sellers get an online store without a monthly bill.

Many small vendors already sell on WhatsApp. They send photo after photo, answer the same price questions all day, and lose orders in a crowded chat.

A proper online store would fix that, but monthly fees put it out of reach for most of them. ShopLink NG gives every seller a clean store page and sends each order to their WhatsApp, so they keep the chat their customers already trust.

What we stand for

Simple first

If a feature needs a tutorial, we keep working on it. Selling should be as easy as posting a status.

Fair to small sellers

No monthly fee to open a store, and no cut taken from your sales. You deal with your customers directly.

Built for Nigeria

Prices in naira, orders on WhatsApp, and light pages that load on weak networks and small phones.
