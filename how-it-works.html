How ShopLink NG works

Open a store in minutes, share one link and get orders or bookings on WhatsApp.

For vendors who sell products

No website skills and no monthly fee.

Create your account

Sign up with your email and choose your store link, like shoplinkng.shop/your-store.

Set up your store

Add your logo, a short description and your WhatsApp number.

Add your products

Upload a photo, set a price and add a category. You can also post products on the Mega Store.

Share and sell

Put your link on your WhatsApp status, Instagram bio and flyers. Orders land in your WhatsApp.

For buyers

No app and no account.

Open the store link

Browse products and prices, or search the Mega Store for sellers across Nigeria.

Pick what you want

Add items to your order and tap Order on WhatsApp.

Pay the seller directly

Confirm the order in the chat, then pay by transfer or on delivery. ShopLink never touches your money.

For service providers

Hair stylists, makeup artists, photographers, caterers, cleaners, tutors and more can take bookings.

Open your booking page

Open Services & bookings from your dashboard. You get your own booking link.

Add your services

Add each service with a price and how long it takes.

Set your hours

Choose your working days, opening time and slot length.

Get bookings

Customers pick a free time. You confirm on WhatsApp with one tap.

Questions

Is ShopLink NG really free to start?

Yes. You can create your store and add products without paying. A small subscription keeps your store open, and you can see it on the Billing page of your dashboard.

Do customers need to download an app?

No. They open your link in their browser and send the order to your WhatsApp.

Who handles the payment?

You do. Customers pay you directly by transfer or on delivery.

What is the Mega Store?

It is the ShopLink marketplace where shoppers browse products from many sellers. Posting there is your choice.

Can I see how my store is doing?

Yes. Your dashboard shows visitors, orders sent, order value and a weekly tip to help you sell more.
